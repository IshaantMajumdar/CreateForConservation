About Us

Create for Conservation is a student-founded organization striving to advocate for a greener future. In a world with new technologies emerging at the expense of natural resources and the environment, we wanted to contribute what we can to preventing further harm to our atmosphere.

We are always looking for more advocates and members to join our cause, so please don't hesitate to contact us!
