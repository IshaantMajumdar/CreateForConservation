Fostering Curiosity

We aim to create advocacy that is both informative and engaging. By using teaching strategies that foster curiosity and participation, we design and plan our lessons with these primary goals in mind.

Training Dexterity

Our art projects, particularly with younger advocates, primarily emphasize developing motor skills. While the activities we offer are straightforward, tasks such as gluing, cutting, and drawing effectively promote fine motor skills.

Building Community

Create for Conservation was founded with community in mind. We hope to provide a safe, accessible space for all ages, as well as an environment that encourages community and connection.

Our Mission

We strive to raise awareness about the fragility and importance of our environment by creating engaging and educational art projects taught by passionate youth advocates for audiences across various communities in America.

We are always looking for more advocates and members to join our cause, so please don't hesitate to contact us!
